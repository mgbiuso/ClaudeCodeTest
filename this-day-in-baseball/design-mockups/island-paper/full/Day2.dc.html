2011 World Series, Game 3

Pujols hits three

From the Wikipedia entry for Oct. 22

Albert Pujols at Busch Stadium in 2008.

Photo: shgmom56, CC BY-SA 2.0

Albert Pujols joins Babe Ruth and Reggie Jackson as the only players to hit three home runs in a World Series game as the St. Louis Cardinals win Game 3, 16-7. Pujols collects a record 14 total bases, ties World Series records for most hits in a game (5) and RBIs (6), and becomes the first player to get hits in four consecutive innings.

In Brief

1974. Yankees trade Bobby Murcer to the Giants for Bobby Bonds.

1981. Joe Torre replaces Bobby Cox as Braves manager.

1985. Bobby Cox quits the Blue Jays to become Braves general manager.

1992. Red Barber dies at 84. He broadcast for the Reds, Dodgers and Yankees.

2007. Tony La Russa agrees to a new two-year deal to manage the Cardinals.

2015. The Dodgers and Don Mattingly part ways with one year left on his deal.
