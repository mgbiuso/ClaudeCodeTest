1928 World Series, Game 1

Yanks strike first

From the Wikipedia entry for Oct. 4

The 1928 New York Yankees won the opener over the St. Louis Cardinals, 4-1.

Photo: Wikimedia Commons, public domain

The Cardinals get just three hits, one of them a home run by Bottomley, as the Yankees take Game 1. The 1928 World Series was the 25th edition, a best-of-seven matchup between the American League champion Yankees and the National League champion Cardinals.

1955 World Series, Game 7

Next year is here

Johnny Podres pitches the Brooklyn Dodgers to their first World Series title, a 2-0 win over the Yankees. Podres is named MVP of the Series, the first time such an award is given.

Podres pitched in the majors from 1953 to 1969, mostly for the Brooklyn and Los Angeles Dodgers, and won four World Series titles.
