The Polo Grounds, New York, 1911 to 1963

4,536 major league games on record · June 28, 1911 to Sept. 18, 1963

The Polo Grounds hosted the Giants for 47 seasons, the Yankees for 11 and the Mets for 2. It was a home run park: baseball's hitters out-homered the league average here in 49 of 49 seasons.
