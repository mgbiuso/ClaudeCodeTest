This Day in Baseball

About, sources and corrections

What this is

A daily page of baseball history. Every date shows games, trades, records, ballparks, farewells and other moments that happened on that day in past years, laid out like the old newspaper sports section.

Where the stories come from

  • Dated entries in Wikipedia's "year in baseball" articles.
  • Wikipedia's "On this day" feed, and sentences from team, ballpark and league articles that state a date.
  • Wikidata, to confirm that a page is about baseball.

Text is condensed from Wikipedia and is available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike license (CC BY-SA). Each story links back to its Wikipedia article.

By the numbers, the wire and the people

Everything in the "By the numbers" and "Around the league" boxes, the game of the day, the decade charts and the year-at-a-glance calendar comes from Retrosheet's data: game logs for every major league game on record back to 1871 and the postseason, the transactions database (1873 to 2020, no longer updated), the people file with debut and final-game dates and Hall of Fame flags, the ejections file, and Retrosheet's own lists of milestones, cycles and other feats. The information used here was obtained free of charge from and is copyrighted by Retrosheet. Interested parties may contact Retrosheet at www.retrosheet.org. The numbers are counted by a program from those files, with no AI involved. Dates from mid-November to March have no games, so those days show the wire and the people instead. A star marks a Hall of Famer.

Headlines

Headlines are written with the help of an AI model, which is only allowed to use facts that appear in the story text. Photos are also checked by an AI model against the story before they are shown.

Photographs

Photos come from Wikimedia Commons. Only freely licensed images are used, and each caption names the photographer and the license, linked to the original file. When no freely licensed photo can be found, the story shows a printed plate with the year instead.

Mistakes

Stories are assembled automatically, so mistakes happen: a wrong photo, a name that was mixed up, or a story that does not belong. To report one, or to ask for a photo or story to be removed, contact the site owner at the address that will be listed here before launch.
